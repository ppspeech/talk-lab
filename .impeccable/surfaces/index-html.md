Scope: the whole Talk Lab app shell and its three sections. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: one CAS clinician; run sessions on iPad with a child beside her, plan on laptop, check on phone; parents see the Family view.
Constraints: keep every function, game, data shape and the shared game module; Australian English; original art only; must not slow her down.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Talk Lab is a deck of articulation cards on a clinic table, not a website with six tabs. It refuses the narrow single-column page with a row of tabs and long scrolling; three sections, one screen each.
OWN-WORLD: cool paper-grey desk ground, crisp white cards with a 1px grey edge and a thick coloured top edge per goal (blue, orange, green, violet); deep ink-navy for primary actions and the selected tab; one family, Atkinson Hyperlegible, bold for headings; soft offset shadows only on the card that is in play.
STORY: she opens a client and is already in Session: today's goal cards sit as a deck on the left, the game and word card fill the right, logging and Finish are one tap; Plan holds goals beside assessment and PMV; Progress holds history beside the family page.
